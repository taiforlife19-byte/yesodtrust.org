Five habits that help

No tech skills needed

They work on every scam, old or new.

Practice these habits →
  1. Pause. Scammers rush you. Real banks and family can wait.
  2. Call back on a number you trust. Never the one in the message.
  3. Never share codes or passwords. Not even with “your bank.”
  4. Gift cards, wires, or crypto? It’s a scam.
  5. Talk it over. Ask someone first. A family code word helps.

If you think you’ve been scammed

It happens to anyone. Act fast.

  • Call your bank, using the number on your card.
  • Change any passwords you shared.
  • Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
  • Get more help on our Trusted Sources page.
