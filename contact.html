Your privacy

This website has no sign-up forms, no accounts, and no tracking. The “Scam or Safe?” game runs entirely in your browser and does not send your answers anywhere.

If you email or call us, we use your message only to reply to you. We don’t sell or share it.

Please don’t send sensitive information. We will never ask for passwords, bank or card numbers, Social Security numbers, or verification codes, and you should never share them with us.

Need help with a possible scam right now?

We are a student-led educational initiative and can’t investigate cases. If you think you’ve been scammed:

  • Call your bank or card company using the number on the back of your card.
  • Tell someone you trust what happened.
  • In the United States, report fraud to the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Yesod Trust Initiative is a student-led educational initiative. The information on this site is for general education and cannot guarantee protection from every scam. When in doubt, contact an organization directly using a phone number or website you already trust.

We will never ask you for passwords, bank details, Social Security numbers, or verification codes.
