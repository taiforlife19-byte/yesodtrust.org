About us

Protecting trust and family connections

We give older adults the knowledge and practical tools to recognize modern scams and stay safer in an increasingly AI-driven world.

Why this matters

Scams have always existed, but they are changing. Today a fraudulent message can look just like one from your bank. A phone call can come from a number that appears familiar. And artificial intelligence (AI), computer software that can produce text, images, and sound, can now imitate a real person’s voice from a short recording.

These scams work by creating urgency and borrowing the trust we place in people and institutions. They can affect anyone. Knowing what to look for, and having a simple plan for checking, makes a real difference.

Who we are

The Yesod Trust Initiative is a student-led educational initiative. We create free learning materials and practice activities that explain modern scams in plain language.

Our name comes from the Hebrew word yesod, meaning “foundation,” because trust is the foundation of every relationship, family, and community.

How we teach

Our approach

  • Respect first Older adults bring a lifetime of judgment and experience. We build on that; we never talk down.
  • Practice, not fear Trying realistic, made-up examples builds confidence that a warning or a news story alone can’t.
  • Plain language We explain technical terms simply and focus on steps anyone can take.
  • Family connection Talking openly about scams with relatives and caregivers helps everyone stay safer together.

Our promise: We will never ask you for passwords, bank or card details, Social Security numbers, or verification codes. If someone claiming to be us asks for these, it is not us.
