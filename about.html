Our mission

A fake text can look exactly like your bank. AI can copy a loved one’s voice from a few seconds of video.

Every one of these scams works the same way: borrow your trust, then rush you. Knowing the signs and having a plan to check stops them.

Who we are

A student-led initiative making free, plain-language tools to beat modern scams.

We’re building a movement of teenagers who help educate older adults. We grew up with this technology, and we can explain it patiently, starting with our own grandparents.

Yesod is Hebrew for “foundation.” Trust is the foundation of every family.

Our approach

  • Respect first Our audience has a lifetime of judgment. We build on it.
  • Practice, not fear Realistic examples build confidence a warning can’t.
  • Plain language No jargon. Just steps anyone can take.
  • Family connection Scams lose their power when families talk about them.
