How it works

  • You’ll see 12 short scenarios, one at a time.
  • Choose Scam or Safe.
  • You’ll get an explanation and a practical tip after every answer.
  • Take as long as you like. There is no timer.

Everything here is made up. All names, companies, phone numbers, and web addresses are fictional. Nothing in the game is a real link, and your answers are not saved or sent anywhere.

    Habits to remember

    • Pause. Real organizations can wait while you check.
    • Call back using a number you already trust.
    • Never share passwords, PINs, or verification codes.
    • Gift cards, wire transfers, and cryptocurrency requests are red flags.
    • Talk it over with someone you trust.
