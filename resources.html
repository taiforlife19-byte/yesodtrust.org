Trusted sources

Where to get real help

Official, free, and verified.

Heads up: scammers pretend to be these groups too. Use the numbers on this page, never ones sent to you.

Call for help

Talk to a person now

Free helplines with trained people on the other end.

  • National Elder Fraud Hotline

    U.S. Department of Justice, for adults 60+ and their families.

    833-FRAUD-11 (833-372-8311) ovc.ojp.gov (opens in a new tab)

  • AARP Fraud Watch Network Helpline

    Free for everyone. Not sure it’s a scam? Ask them.

    Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern

    877-908-3360 aarp.org/fraudhelpline (opens in a new tab)

  • Eldercare Locator

    Finds local help for older adults and caregivers.

    800-677-1116 eldercare.acl.gov (opens in a new tab)

Report a scam

Tell the authorities

Report even if you didn’t lose money.

  • Federal Trade Commission (FTC)

    Report any scam in the U.S.

    ReportFraud.ftc.gov (opens in a new tab)

  • FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)

    For online, email, tech-support, and investment scams.

    ic3.gov (opens in a new tab)

  • Social Security scams

    Someone pretending to be Social Security.

    oig.ssa.gov/report (opens in a new tab)

  • Your state attorney general

    File a complaint in your state.

    Find your state’s office (opens in a new tab)

  • Not sure where to report?

    Answer a few questions, get the right agency.

    usa.gov/where-report-scams (opens in a new tab)

Protect your identity

If your information or money was taken

First, call your bank using the number on your card.

  • IdentityTheft.gov

    Get a step-by-step recovery plan from the FTC.

    877-438-4338 IdentityTheft.gov (opens in a new tab)

  • Freeze your credit

    Free. Stops new accounts in your name. Call all three.

    Equifax: 800-685-1111
    Experian: 888-397-3742
    TransUnion: 888-909-8872

    FTC guide to credit freezes (opens in a new tab)

Fewer scam calls

Cut down on unwanted calls

Fewer calls, fewer chances to get scammed.

  • National Do Not Call Registry

    Stops sales calls within 31 days. Any that still come are suspicious.

    Call from the phone you want to register.

    888-382-1222 DoNotCall.gov (opens in a new tab)
